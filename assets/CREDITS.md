# Asset credits

## Scenery (public/scenery.js)

No new files were downloaded for the scenery. It reuses the CC0 Poly Haven textures already in `assets/tex`
(bark, planks, metal, rock, snow; https://polyhaven.com, CC0) and draws everything else procedurally at load time
on canvases: the conifer needle-spray atlas, the orange snow-fence mesh, and every printed graphic (the
SNOWLINE PARK banner and logo, feather and pole flags, park/jump/lane signs, building signs). All of those
designs are original to this game; no real brands, logos or other games' assets are used.
